# General Company Information

PT Bumi Nirwana Estate, along with its legal project entity PT. Margojoyo Anugrah Persada, is a property developer focused on providing quality housing in Lumajang. Our vision is to help the community, especially millennials and new families, own their dream home by offering properties that are high-quality, comfortable, strategic, and affordable. Our projects are located in key areas such as Klampokarum (Tekung), Sumberejo (Sukodono), and the city center in Ditotrunana. Our marketing office is located at Jalan Achmad Yani No. 172, Lumajang.

# KPR Process and Requirements Information

The Booking Fee (Uang Tanda Jadi or UTJ) to secure a unit is around Rp 750,000 to Rp 1,000,000 for subsidized houses and is non-refundable in case of unilateral cancellation. However, the Down Payment (DP) is refundable if the KPR application is rejected due to a failed BI Checking. The selling price of the house includes the IMB/PBG (building permits), electricity installation, and a drilled well. General requirements for a KPR application include photocopies of KTP, KK, NPWP, passport-sized photos, marriage certificate, the last 3 months of salary slips, and a letter of employment. For civil servants (PNS), the latest SK document is required. For entrepreneurs, SIUP/TDP or SKU documents are needed.
